# Historical Provenance: The Safern Family & Tzofnat Paneach

## Executive Summary

This document records the historical provenance connecting the **Safern family** to the preservation, publication, and digitization of the manuscripts of the **Rogatchover Gaon** (R. Yosef Rosen of Dvinsk, *ztn"l*, author of *Tzofnat Paneach*).

---

## Historical Record

1. **Rescue of the Manuscripts (1939–1942)**:
   Following the passing of the Rogatchover Gaon in 1936, his daughter **Rachel Citron** and his student/successor **Rabbi Yisroel Alter Safern-Fuchs** organized and microfilmed thousands of pages of unprinted *Tzofnat Paneach* manuscripts in Dvinsk, Latvia. Facing Nazi occupation, they mailed the microfilms weekly to **R. Zvi Hirsch Safern** in the Bronx, New York, successfully saving the works before their martyrdom in 1942.

2. **Catalyst for Publication (William Safern A"H)**:
   For years after WWII, the microfilms sat unexamined in New York. **William Safern A"H** (father of Eric Safern) convinced **Rabbi Menachem Mendel Kasher** (*Tzofnat Paneach Institute*) and **Rabbi Pinchas Teitz** (of Elizabeth, NJ) to decipher, edit, and publish the microfilmed manuscripts.

3. **Digital Pipeline Continuity (Eric Safern)**:
   The diagnostic test file used for building out this pipeline—[`KiddushinRogochovDraft.pdf`](file:///Users/ericsafern/work/yad-malachi/yad-malachi-pipeline/KiddushinRogochovDraft.pdf) / [`test_page.pdf`](file:///Users/ericsafern/work/yad-malachi/yad-malachi-pipeline/test_page.pdf) (*Tzofnat Paneach* on Tractate Kiddushin 2a)—represents a direct continuation of this family legacy: moving the Rogatchover's writings from microfilms and print into high-precision, linkable digital texts.
