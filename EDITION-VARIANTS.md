**This is collation, not a correction queue. Nothing here is a defect and nothing here may be applied.** The corpus transcribes Berlin, 1851/2 - the second printing, not the Livorno 1766-7 original; these rows describe a DIFFERENT printing (Jerusalem 1975/6 (Rashi script), via Dicta RashiOCR). Where Berlin prints an abbreviation, the Berlin abbreviation is what the corpus must keep - that is what the reviewer ruled in item 0AQ.

Only positions where **every Berlin-reading engine agrees with the corpus** are listed, so the Berlin ink is not in question at any of them. The far larger set of cross-edition differences is deliberately excluded as mostly Dicta's own misreads - see the header of `pipeline/build_collation_report.py` for the measurement.
